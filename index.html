Green World Roleplaying System

A classless, setting-independent tabletop roleplaying system built around freedom, flexibility, and character growth.

GWRS is currently in development.

About GWRS

Green World Roleplaying System is a tabletop roleplaying system designed to give players extensive freedom in character creation, combat, and progression.

Instead of restricting characters to traditional classes, GWRS allows characters to develop through their abilities, skills, feats, knowledge, equipment, and experiences.

The system is designed to support both detailed mechanical interactions and flexible Game Master adjudication, allowing players to attempt actions beyond a fixed list of options.
