Green World Roleplaying System

A setting-independent tabletop roleplaying system with no level and no class, built around freedom, flexibility and character growth.

GWRS is currently in development.

About GWRS

Green World Roleplaying System is a tabletop roleplaying system designed to give players extensive freedom in character creation, combat and progression.

The system is designed to support both detailed mechanical interactions and flexible Game Master rulings, allowing players to attempt actions beyond a fixed list of options.
